# MODULO: SURVIVE — World & Lore Bible

## The Premise

Reality here was **built**, not born. Eons ago the **Modular civilization** discovered that
their universe repeats — space folds back on itself in vast congruent strata they called
*moduli*. They learned to cut doors between congruent points, wrote resonance into stone, and
assembled a lattice of eight aligned worlds with the Overworld as its keystone.

Then the alignment slipped. A hairline drift — one part in a trillion per cycle — compounded
until the strata sheared. The event is remembered only in fragments carved on obelisks:
**The Remainder**. Cities fell through their own foundations. The Moduli sealed the doors,
scattered their machines, and vanished, leaving wardens of stone to keep the seals.

You are a survivor of a much smaller catastrophe — a fallen sky-vessel — waking on the
keystone world with nothing. The drift has never stopped. Nights grow strange. Static crawls
across a red moon. Sometimes the sun goes out at noon. The lattice is failing, and everything
that made the Moduli powerful is still down there, humming in the dark.

## The Eight Strata

| Stratum | What it was | What it is now |
|---|---|---|
| **Overworld** | the keystone, a garden world | wild, healing, haunted at night |
| **Ancient** | the Moduli capital stratum | ochre ruins under dust, vaults intact |
| **Crystal** | their resonance farm | light trapped mid-refraction, singing caverns |
| **Frozen** | a stasis archive | permafrost holding what they froze on purpose |
| **Molten** | the forge stratum | basalt seas, foundries still lit |
| **Machine** | automated industry | factories running for no one, gone feral |
| **Sky** | observatories on island chains | gale-torn archipelagos, courts of wind |
| **Void** | the failed ninth alignment | inverted, silent, hungry — the drift's source |

## The Wardens & Powers

- **Warden of the First Modulus** — the last functioning seal-keeper. It does not hate you;
  it simply cannot tell a survivor from a thief. Defeating it yields the resonance knowledge
  to *build doors again*.
- **Chronophage** — a parasite of stalled time that surfaces when the sun fails (the Umbral
  Veil). Eats hours. Drops them, crystallized.
- **Borea, the Stilled Heart** — the Frozen archive's custodian, grief given a glacier.
- **Pyraxis the Undermelt** — a forge-current that refused to be banked.
- **F4BR-1K, Foundry Tyrant** — the Machine stratum's production supervisor, still meeting
  quota with survivor-shaped raw material.
- **The Zephyr Court** — three wind-sovereigns fought as one encounter above the islands.
- **The Refractor** — a being of misfolded light in the Crystal deep.
- **The Null Sovereign** — what waits in the Void: the Remainder itself, wearing a crown.

## The Survivors (playable)

Robin the Builder, Leif the Hunter, Jovan the Explorer, Leonidas the Warrior, Erim the
Engineer, Till the Gatherer, Lenni the Scholar and Tusya the Merchant — the eight-person crew
of the survey vessel *Quotient*, which clipped a drift-shear in low orbit. Each brought his
profession down with him; none brought much else.

## The NPCs

- **Ordo the Merchant** — claims he's been "between strata" for two hundred years and that
  your coins are antiques. Prices accordingly.
- **Vessa the Herbalist** — maps the drift by where the flowers grow wrong.
- **Brann the Tinker** — ex-salvager who refuses to enter the Machine stratum again, but will
  absolutely sell you the tools to try.
- **Kael the Nomad** — walks the surface reading obelisks. Sends you to the ones he can't reach.

## Naming & Tone

Original nomenclature throughout: *moduli, drift-shear, resonance, Remainder, strata*.
The tone is quiet wonder over horror — a broken machine-world that is beautiful in its decay,
dangerous mostly because nobody is maintaining it anymore.
